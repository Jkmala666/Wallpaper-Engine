## 🛠️ Troubleshooting common issues

If you encounter problems, check these common fixes first.

*   Background not showing: Ensure the application is active in your system tray. If the icon is missing, launch the program again from the shortcut on your desktop.
*   Performance issues: Open the settings menu and change the quality settings to Medium or Low. This reduces the load on your graphics card.
*   Wallpapers stop moving: Some backgrounds pause when you have windows open on your desktop. You can change this behavior in the general settings tab.
*   Sound is too loud: You can mute individual wallpapers or the entire application within the volume mixer settings.
